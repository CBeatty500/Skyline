Scientific Methodology & Verification

Trend lines are derived using Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression over the full ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis dataset.

  • Dataset: ECMWF ERA5 (1940–Present) via Open-Meteo Historical Archive API.
  • Warming Slope: Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ) / Σ(x − x̄)², fitted to complete calendar years only (partial years are excluded to avoid seasonal bias).
  • Diurnal Asymmetry: Minimum nighttime temperatures typically exhibit accelerated warming rates due to boundary layer moisture retention.
